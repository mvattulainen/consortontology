# Source and Licensing Notes

## Source hierarchy

1. The CONSORT 2025 Statement and official expanded checklist are the normative baseline.
2. The CONSORT 2025 Explanation and Elaboration is interpretive.
3. The official SPIRIT–CONSORT website supplies publication and extension context.

## Attribution

Official checklist wording in this vault is marked in source callouts or `source_text` fields and attributed to:

Hopewell S, Chan A-W, Collins GS, Hróbjartsson A, Moher D, Schulz KF, et al. *CONSORT 2025 statement: updated guideline for reporting randomised trials*. BMJ. 2025;389:e081123. DOI: 10.1136/bmj-2024-081123.

The official statement and expanded checklist identify a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Reuse must preserve attribution. The Explanation and Elaboration record retains its publisher-level license note rather than assuming identical terms.
