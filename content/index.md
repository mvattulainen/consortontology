# CONSORT 2025 Guideline Ontology

This site formalizes Layer A of CONSORT 2025: the reporting guideline itself. It contains the guideline metamodel, 42 checklist items, reporting requirements, validation rules, controlled vocabularies, and a first-class trial-domain model.

## Start exploring

1. [[00 Start Here|Start here]] for the suggested reading path.
2. [[01 Ontology Primer|Ontology primer]] for the core vocabulary.
3. [[02 CONSORT 2025 Ontology Overview|Ontology overview]] for the model at a glance.
4. [[11 Trial Domain Model|Trial domain model]] for trial-related classes.
5. [[09 Checklist Index|Checklist index]] to browse all 42 leaf items.

> [!note]
> This ontology models what CONSORT asks authors to report. It does not assess reporting completeness, methodological quality, or risk of bias for individual studies.
